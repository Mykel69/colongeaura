The fine print

Shipping, returns & privacy.

Last updated November 26, 2026

Shipping Returns Privacy

Shipping

Cost

Shipping is a flat $6 per order, and free on orders of $50 or more (before shipping).

Processing time

Orders usually ship within 3–5 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays).

Delivery

We currently ship within the United States only, through USPS ground service. Fragrance contains alcohol, so it cannot travel by air. Delivery time after shipping depends on your location and USPS.

We'll email you a tracking number once your order is on its way. Please double-check your address at checkout, and contact us right away if you spot a mistake.

If your tracking stops updating, or shows delivered but you don't have your package, contact us and we'll help look into it.

Returns

All sales are final

Because decants are rebottled, personal-use products, we don't accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind or scent preference.

Damaged, leaked, or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or leaking, or you received the wrong item, contact us within 7 days of delivery. Include:

  • The email address used for your order
  • A short description of the problem
  • A photo of the item and packaging

We'll make it right with a replacement or a refund. Refunds go back to your original payment method and can take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to run the shop, and we don't sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • Orders: your name, email, shipping address, delivery notes, and what you bought.
  • Payments: card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page. We never receive your full card number.
  • Accounts (optional): your email, password, and order history. Passwords are stored in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Newsletter and contact form: your email address, plus your name and message if you write to us.
  • Technical data: the services that deliver this site may log basic details such as your IP address and browser type.

How we use it

To process and ship orders, send order, tracking, and account emails, answer your questions, send updates if you join the list, and keep business records.

Services that help us

Information is shared only as needed with:

  • Stripe (payments)
  • Cloudflare (checkout processing)
  • Supabase (accounts and order history)
  • Resend and EmailJS (emails)
  • Formspree (newsletter and contact forms)
  • Google (our shop inbox and website fonts)
  • GitHub Pages and jsDelivr (website hosting and code delivery)
  • USPS (delivery)

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Browser storage

Your browser's local storage keeps your bag and keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising or analytics trackers. Stripe may set its own cookies on its checkout page.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information, close your account, or take you off our list. Some order records may need to be kept for tax or legal reasons. This site is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If these policies change, we'll update this page and the date above.
